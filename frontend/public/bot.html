About CushLabsBot

If you found CushLabsBot in your server logs, this page explains what it is, how it behaves, and how to turn it away.

What it is

CushLabsBot is the crawler behind the CushLabs AI Web Scraper, a site-analysis tool. Someone asked it to review a site, usually their own or one they are advising on, and it reads a limited number of pages to report on SEO and technical issues. It does not harvest email addresses, copy content for republication, or feed a search index.

It identifies itself on every request:

User-Agent: CushLabsBot/1.0 (+https://scraper.cushlabs.ai/bot.html)

How it behaves

  • robots.txt is honoured. Disallow rules for CushLabsBot or * are respected, and Crawl-delay slows the crawler down.
  • One request at a time. Requests to a site go out one after another, never in parallel, at a default of one request per second or slower.
  • It backs off when asked. A 429 or 503 response, or a bot challenge, pauses the crawler. Retry-After is respected. After three in a row it stops the crawl.
  • It stays small. Crawls are capped in pages and in time, and link checks are limited to a few hundred per crawl.
  • It does not log in, fill forms, or run your JavaScript. It reads what an anonymous visitor could read.

How to block it

Add this to your robots.txt. The crawler checks it before fetching any page. 

User-agent: CushLabsBot
Disallow: /

To slow it down instead of blocking it:

User-agent: CushLabsBot
Crawl-delay: 10
